# GDSSA Research Scope and Claim Boundaries

## Status

Conceptual hypothesis v0.1. No claim of academic priority, universal novelty, or experimentally demonstrated superiority is made yet.

## Primary research question

> When a sufficiently capable AI is given a goal, boundaries, access to evidence and tools, and observable success criteria, when should method-design responsibility move from the human to the AI?

The narrower experimental question is:

> At what model capability, task type, evidence quality, and authority boundary does human procedural guidance stop helping and begin constraining goal achievement?

## Unit of comparison

The first controlled comparison is not "agent vs chatbot". It is the **allocation of method-design responsibility**.

### BARE-GOAL

The human supplies:

- goal;
- boundaries;
- authority;
- available environment;
- success evidence.

The AI determines the method.

### HUMAN-GUIDED

The human supplies the same information plus:

- decomposition;
- questions to ask;
- suggested tools;
- workflow;
- implementation hints.

## Claims this research may eventually support

If evidence accumulates, GDSSA may support claims about:

- lower human intervention for equivalent or better real-world outcomes;
- improved adaptation to unprepared tasks;
- better reuse of persistent capabilities and operational learning;
- a shift in optimal human contribution from method design toward goal, authority, values, and real-world observation;
- conditions under which procedural prompting becomes a ceiling for stronger models.

## Claims not currently supported

This repository does not currently establish that:

- GDSSA is the first or only framework with these ideas;
- goal-only delegation is always superior;
- autonomous agents are obsolete;
- humans can be removed from consequential decisions;
- current models can reliably self-structure arbitrary real-world work;
- a generated architecture is safe merely because the model proposed it;
- a working general GDSSA implementation already exists.

## Hard boundaries

Self-structuring means freedom to redesign the **working path**, not freedom to expand authority.

External action boundaries must remain enforceable outside model reasoning where possible:

- credentials;
- permissions;
- budgets;
- irreversible actions;
- data boundaries;
- production promotion;
- safety and legal constraints.

## What would falsify or weaken the hypothesis

Evidence against GDSSA would include repeated findings that, under matched tools and models:

- human-designed procedural decomposition consistently improves real outcomes;
- BARE-GOAL causes substantially more cost, delay, or architecture thrashing;
- models fail to discover critical unknowns without explicit human prompting;
- self-selected tools and structures are less reliable than human-selected ones;
- human intervention does not decrease as capabilities and operational memory accumulate.

Negative results should be preserved, not reframed as success.
