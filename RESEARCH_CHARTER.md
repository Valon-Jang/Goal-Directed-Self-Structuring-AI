# GDSSA — Continuing Research Charter

Date: 2026-09-13. Authority: the repository owner explicitly designated this repository as the AI's ongoing experimental workspace and asked for continuous development beyond the current state. This authorizes relevant research design, bounded experiments, code, evidence and handoffs here; it is not ownership transfer, unlimited spending, external publication of private data, or permission expansion.

## North star

Develop a system that achieves human-governed goals with progressively less unnecessary human method design, while preserving quality, safety, observability and cost discipline as tasks, tools and models change.

The objective is not to protect a specific prompt, architecture, model, benchmark score or preferred hypothesis. A better future method may simplify or replace today's implementation. Preserve the evidence and lessons that justify change, not every historical procedure.

## What counts as progress

1. A previously unexecutable experiment becomes executable and observable.
2. A causal uncertainty is reduced by a controlled test, including a negative result.
3. A useful capability works on a changed task, not only its construction example.
4. An unnecessary component is removed without losing verified outcomes.
5. A regression is detected, contained, explained and prevented from silent promotion.
6. The user's preparation, review, repair or supervision burden falls under comparable quality and cost.

New files, more agent roles, longer prompts and repeated runs of a known fixture are not progress by themselves.

## Two independent research lanes

**Instrument lane:** executable environment, real-model transport, boundaries, provenance, active-human-time measurement, resumable trials and reproducible analysis. It may proceed while human participants are unavailable. Label software tests, scripted fixtures and real-model developmental smoke separately.

**Causal comparison lane:** genuinely human-authored initial methods versus BARE-GOAL with matched facts, permissions, tools, models and adaptation freedom. Do not fill a missing human role by role-playing it. Preserve the E001 question; AI-designed procedural controls are separately named exploratory conditions.

A blocked causal comparison must not halt all useful research, but instrument work must not be misrepresented as the comparison's result.

## Continuous-development experiment sequence

E001 remains the allocation-of-method-design study. E0.1 now develops actual model execution and measurement. Later candidates must test (a) new inputs, (b) changed schema/policy, (c) stale or defective retained capabilities, (d) a replaced model/runtime and (e) when starting fresh is better than reusing.

For each candidate: state the uncertainty and expected falsifier; preserve an immutable baseline; change the smallest relevant factor; test on development cases; freeze before a new evaluation slice; report all attempted cases and costs; promote, reject, or retain as unresolved. Reusing evaluation feedback for tuning retires that slice from future holdout status. A new task generation seed is not sufficient evidence of semantic novelty when it only shuffles rows.

Track a longitudinal frontier of quality, human labor, compute, rework and transfer. Do not claim monotonic progress by dropping hard tasks, hiding failed runs, changing denominators or lowering acceptance criteria. New directions can expand the charter, but comparisons within an experiment remain frozen and changes are recorded prospectively.

## Durable knowledge and capability lifecycle

Persist validated findings, exact negative fingerprints, limitations, reusable artifacts, dependencies, budgets and the next highest-value uncertainty. Do not store private chain-of-thought or source secrets. A retained capability needs provenance, tested scope, actual invocation and current evidence. Candidate -> scoped verification -> degraded -> repair/retest or retire. No permanent certification and no automatic production deployment.

The model and method are replaceable. Goals, authority boundaries and evidence remain human-governed. No experiment may silently expand its own permissions or rewrite its own acceptance criteria to manufacture success.

## Execution and visibility boundaries

This repository is public; only generic code, synthetic tasks and non-sensitive research evidence are eligible for commits. Actual participant identities, private task data, credentials, unpublished held-out inputs and other private repositories are excluded. Keep the original theory and past results intact; work on research branches with reviewable diffs.

Prefer existing standard CPU resources and public checksum-pinned models. New paid services, paid GPUs, production/customer actions and broader sharing require separate authorization. Each execution has a bounded scope, output, stop condition and durable status. This charter creates no unattended infinite loop or scheduled task. In an interactive session, execute and report the current bounded cycle; do not promise invisible background progress.

## Current next decision

Can the instrument acquire a pinned public small model, start a fresh local-only inference process, complete a real action/tool/response cycle under bounded resources, and recover verifiable evidence? This is E0.1 and does not test whether small-model results generalize to capable frontier models or real office work.

After that, improve the weakest evidenced boundary rather than growing the architecture by default. Keep genuine human participation as an explicit causal-study gate while progressing independent instrumentation.
