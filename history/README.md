# History

This directory preserves superseded hypotheses, terminology, and experimental directions when they remain useful for understanding why the research changed.

## Initial lineage

The GDSSA operating model emerged from a sequence of ideas:

1. Socratic requirement discovery — AI discovers questions the human may not know to ask.
2. Question-Driven Deepening — ask only what can materially change the next decision.
3. Root Engineering — keep validated project truth outside replaceable model/runtime context.
4. AX Scout / Builder / Operator — AI discovers transformation opportunities, acquires capabilities, and performs transformed work.
5. Future-Improving Architecture — persist stable goals/evidence/capabilities while letting stronger future models redesign the path.
6. GDSSA — move method-design responsibility itself from the human toward the AI, while the human retains goal, reality, values, and authority.
